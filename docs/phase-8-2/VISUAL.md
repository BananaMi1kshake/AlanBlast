# Phase 8.2 screenshot study

The developer supplied six portrait screenshots. The visible layout is the source for this phase. The Phase 8.1 AlanBlox build at `7962af3` is the comparison baseline.

| Reference screen | Observed in the supplied screenshot | AlanBlox design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Studio splash | Centered studio mark on a spare vertical blue gradient. | Alan Studio's existing mark, with a warm light halo for contrast against the blue. |
| Game splash | Large upper-half wordmark, small block motif farther down, and open space. | Existing AlanBlox wordmark and an original blue-and-amber corner motif. |
| Main menu | Large upper logo and a low stack of broad, colorful buttons. | Teal Classic and apricot Adventure buttons, with compact resume, Daily and collection links. |
| Gameplay | Warm grain, gold best score at upper left, compact tools at upper right, separate centered score, framed dark square board and open piece area. | Honey-walnut Classic scene with original grain, board and block styling. Compact navigation stays accessible on phones. |
| Settings | Warm frame with a dark inset, an upper-right close action, and aligned rows. | Toasted frame, game controls, labeled switches, and local save/install/help actions. |
| Material picker | Small swatch and two controls close to the gameplay toolbar. | A compact AlanBlox dialog with a live swatch and labeled Apply and Next controls. |

The screenshots establish composition, relative scale and color direction. They do not establish startup duration, transition timing, touch behavior, motion or physical-device performance. Timing choices come from the AlanBlox baseline and Chromium measurements. The available Alan Studio and AlanBlox assets establish the distinct visual identity. Source screenshots are kept out of the checkout and the offline app shell.
