How to Play

Drag one of the three pieces onto the board. Complete a row or column to clear it. Use all three pieces to get a new hand. Clear again within three moves to keep your combo going.

You can also choose a piece and tap its top-left target cell, or use the arrow keys and Enter.

About AlanBlox

AlanBlox by Alan Studio is a free, offline block puzzle. Each new hand has at least one solution, and your scores stay on this device.
